Here's the new Clips flow in Content Lab. With no clips yet, the Clips card opens a creation form: pick a clip length, add optional instructions or a suggested prompt, choose a language, and hit Generate. A loading state runs while the AI works, then the clips land as drafts. Each thumbnail shows where the clip sits in the video, and Reuse prompt brings back the settings used. Selecting a clip opens a preview to trim, save, or delete. And from Summary and chapters, we're testing a shortcut: one click turns a chapter into a draft clip, with no prompt to reuse.
